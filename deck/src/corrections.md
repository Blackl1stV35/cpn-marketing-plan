## Wording
- Team proposals carry [Proposed] where the report does; assumptions, calculations, interpretations and assessments are labelled team assumption / team calculation / team assessment / inference. Company-reported results are labelled company-reported.
- Plain English, short sentences. Headlines are full sentences with the key number and the so-what.
- Retired sources, never cited: Fitch Ratings, Khaosod, Kaohoon, NESDC, "POSITIONING 2024".
- The base case does not meet F1 (growth 6.6% against 7.0%; margin 34.6% against the 35.5% floor); only the optimistic case does. The slides must say so plainly and must not claim that the plan "pays for itself" unless Chapter 5 supports it exactly.
