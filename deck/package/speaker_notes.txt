By Q2 2026, CPN had 45 shopping centres and 16 community malls. Three openings are dated for 2027: The Orchard and The Central Phaholyothin in the first quarter, CenTRal cENtrAL retail in the second. S7 | Research and indicators | 0:35 | P4 | 75 words
We propose to measure Gen Z and young adults by survey (ages 20–29) and member data (18–29), with baselines by 31 March 2027 to set targets against our data.

We define Gen Z and young adults as ages 18 to 29. Our survey reaches only people aged 20 and over, the age of majority, so it measures 20 to 29; member data cover active members 18 to 29. We found no CPN age split of visits, so we set a baseline by 31 March 2027. The only published comparator, 14.7 percent for ages 13 to 24, is another band. Four indicators track proposed targets.

S8 | Break-even + sales forecast | 0:55 | P4 | 103 words
Base-case growth is 6.6% in FY2027E against the 7.0% goal, and the extra revenue does not repay the ฿560 million a year of net-new spend by FY2028E.

We add 560 million baht a year of net-new marketing. Only 60 percent of extra revenue is kept, so we need 933 million more a year. The tactics are the 240 million that earns modelled revenue; they need 400 million and repay in 1.2 years. The full 560 million a year does not. In 2028 it needs 67 percent of any gain to reach CPN's revenue; we assumed 50. The tactics need only 29 percent. Base-case growth is 6.6 percent in 2027 and 6.9 in 2028, short of 7. Only the optimistic case passes, by definition. Our fifth speaker covers expenses and profit.

S9 | Expense forecast + profit | 0:55 | P5 | 103 words
The plan costs margin: base-case net margin is 34.6% in FY2027E against our 35.5% floor, a ฿558 million shortfall, so F1 is not met.

We assume CPN pays all 560 million baht of net-new marketing. That lifts administrative costs to 17.4 percent of revenue in 2027. The conservative case crosses our 18 percent trigger. The base case margin falls to 34.6 percent, against our 35.5 percent floor: 558 million short. Without the plan the margin would be 35.2 percent, also below our floor, so the plan costs about 0.7 points. Our floor is FY2025's margin, which includes 3.2 points of one-off Rama 2 gains. Only the optimistic case holds our floor, and it is a target by definition. So the base case does not meet objective F1.

S10 | Implementation + organization | 0:50 | P5 | 103 words
We propose to release money in five gates from 15 December 2026, inside a ฿560 million net-new cap a year; a failed gate holds or cuts the next release.

We propose releasing money in five gates from 15 December 2026. Net-new spend is capped at 560 million baht. A failed gate holds or cuts the next release. Gates zero and one test readiness: set-up and baseline. From gate two, we test results. Gate zero releases the first 155 million baht for Gen Z, tourist and CENTRAL X programmes. Gates one and two release the second half; gate three, the year-end season. Gate four, on 31 January 2028, decides the 2028 repeat. A proposed Plan Office under CPN's chief marketing officer owns the dashboard and the gates. Our sixth speaker covers the risks.

------------------------------------------------------------

S11 | Contingency planning | 0:50 | P6 | 103 words
If 2020 repeated, CPN's own 51.6% core profit fall would cut FY2027E profit by ฿10.5bn, more than marketing can absorb, so credit is the constraint to watch.

The ratings are our judgement. Only household debt is rated high on both likelihood and impact. In our assessment, Gen Z disengagement is our biggest exposure. Our thesis rests on Gen Z, so gate three tests it. Our response is a three-step ladder. Amber freezes some spending. Red suspends lines and trims the flagship seasons by 20 percent. Crisis suspends most lines and runs tenant relief. A closure like 2020 would trigger Crisis. CPN's own core profit fell 51.6 percent then; the same fall on our base is 10.5 billion baht. Marketing cuts cannot absorb that, so credit is the constraint we watch.

------------------------------------------------------------

S12 | Three takeaways | 0:30 | P6 | 55 words
We propose ฿560 million of net-new marketing; the base case misses F1 (6.6% growth, 34.6% margin), and gates and a response ladder control the spend.

On our assumptions, we propose 560 million baht net-new, inside a 2,760 million gross ceiling. Our base case misses F1. The 560 million a year is not repaid by 2028. Only the optimistic case meets F1, a target by definition. We propose gates and a response ladder to control spending. Thank you; we welcome questions.
