## 4. Marketing Tactics

This chapter turns the Section 3 objectives into tactics for FY2027 (1 January to 31 December 2027), in support of the team thesis that CPN is using its capital to capture Gen Z share through sustainable positioning. Items marked [Proposed] are team proposals, not CPN guidance. Results that CPN reports for itself are labelled company-reported. In the FY2025 One Report, the 2Q26 MD&A and the releases reviewed, CPN publishes no customer-segment data (age, nationality or Gen Z share), although its business-segment revenue (rental and services, real estate sales, hotel) is published (Central Pattana Investor Relations, 2026, p. 15), so Section 4.5 builds a Gen Z baseline in the first 90 days and every Gen Z target is set against it. Owners are functions that Section 6.2 maps to the organisation.

The Section 3.4 order of target markets is unchanged: working adults and families are the Primary segment, served by the base programmes and measured through P2 and S2, while Gen Z and young adults, the Secondary segment, receive the largest share of incremental tactics because of M1 and the team thesis.

### 4.1 Product

This section serves M1, M3, M4, M5 and F1. Gen Z and young adults receive the most incremental effort, working adults and families (Primary) are served by P2, and local brands by LEAD. Leasing and tenant mix owns it, with Development, Operations and Sustainability.

Central Northville opened in early July 2026 (CPN release dated 3 July 2026) with a Japanese-style sports-entertainment centre. The release reports a five-fold rise in Gen Z tennis-related spending without naming its data source or method, so it is company-reported and untested (Central Pattana, 2026e). CenTRal cENtrAL in Siam Square, above ฿11 billion with retail targeted for 2Q27, defines its youth audience by mindset, not age, and does not use the term Gen Z (Central Pattana, 2026f). The Orchard, an extension of Central Northville due in Q1 2027, offers fresh produce, a morning community destination and home and living offerings, so the team reads it as a family product, not a Gen Z one (Central Pattana, 2026e).

LEAD reported in March 2025 over 240 brands supported and over ฿3.8 billion put into the economy after six years (Central Pattana, 2025e). For The 1 BIZ, CPN states that partner brands reached a growth rate 2.7 times that of general brands and average sales boosts of up to 10% within six months, without defining the comparison group, so both are company-reported (Central Pattana, 2024e). Sustainability features exist but are uneven. The One Report says 42 shopping centres have electric-vehicle charging and a recycling campaign at centralwOrld run through The 1 had 3,249 participants, yet only 7.2% of the portfolio by floor area is green-certified, all company-reported (Central Pattana Investor Relations, 2026, pp. 126, 140). The framework's 13% green-building milestone from 2026 uses a different base (assets), so messaging must stay within certified fact (Central Pattana, 2024d).

### 4.2 Pricing

This section serves F1, M1, M2, M3 and M4 for B2B tenants, Gen Z and tourists. Leasing and Finance own rent; CRM and Marketing own The 1 offers and event access.

CPN's rental and services line was ฿43,721 million in 2025, which is 87.38% of revenue from operations of ฿50,034 million (Central Pattana Investor Relations, 2026, p. 15) and 82.5% of the ฿53,009 million total revenue (team calculation from Central Pattana, 2026a). Leases carry an initial non-cancellable period of three years with fixed and variable rent (Central Pattana Investor Relations, 2026, p. 441), and CPN says tenants have gained bargaining power (p. 66). In the FY2025 One Report, the 2Q26 MD&A and the releases reviewed (a team text search), CPN publishes no rent per square metre, fixed-to-variable split, percentage-of-sales rate, gross-profit-share model or tariff for event and casual space, so the team proposes no rent levels. Prime retail asking rent in Bangkok's Central Retail District was ฿3,769 per square metre a month in Q3 2026, stable on Q2, with prime retail vacancy of 3.63% there (Cushman & Wakefield, 2026, p. 1); this is a market figure, not a CPN rent.

The 1 points last two years from the purchase year, are used oldest first and generally cannot be refunded once redeemed, except when a purchase is returned, and promotional points may expire differently (The1 Central Limited, n.d.-b); official earn and burn rates were not available, so the team states none. State Your Style used capped vouchers: it offered a ฿400 e-voucher per ฿4,500 of cumulative spend, limited to 9,800 vouchers (Central Pattana, 2026j), an effective rate of 8.9% and a ceiling of about ฿3.9 million (team calculation). The events reviewed gate access by registration or spend, such as ฿3,000 for a chance at a fan meet (Central Pattana, 2025a) and CENTRAL X registration for the centralwOrld countdown (The Thaiger, 2025); the only ticket prices found were ฿200 early-bird and ฿299 at the door for a Pride night run (Central Pattana, 2026l). Tourists who register for The 1 Tourist at 14 centres receive a free welcome package with discounts of up to 40%, which CPN may change without notice (Central Pattana, 2026k).

### 4.3 Distribution (Place)

This section serves F1, M1, M2 and M3 for all four segments, with incremental effort weighted to Gen Z and tourists. Development and Operations own the network; Marketing and Digital own tourist and O2O channels.

CPN ended FY2025 with 44 shopping centres and 16 community malls and reports about 510 million visits a year, including more than 70 million international (Central Pattana, 2026a); by Q2 2026 the count was 45 and 16 on about 2.4 million square metres (Central Pattana, 2026g, p. 2), and the CFO placed the centres in 23 provinces in November 2025 (Central Pattana, 2025c). The ฿110 billion plan for 2026–2030 targets 33 mixed-use projects by 2030, with no project-by-project split in the releases (Central Group, 2026). FY2027 openings are company targets, not completions: The Orchard in Q1 2027 (Central Pattana, 2026e); the project that the 2Q26 MD&A lists as "The Central", which the team reads as The Central Phaholyothin, in 1Q27 with about 100,000 square metres of net leasable area; and CenTRal cENtrAL retail in 2Q27 with offices in 4Q27 (Central Pattana, 2026f, 2026g, p. 7). CPN's October 2025 release targeted completion in Q4 2026 (Central Pattana, 2026b) and the One Report lists December 2026 (Central Pattana Investor Relations, 2026, p. 30), so, on the team reading, the date has moved, although Cushman & Wakefield's Q3 2026 table of retail projects still lists a 152,000 sq m "The Central" by Central Group for Q4 2026 (Cushman & Wakefield, 2026, p. 2). TRIS Rating expects phased openings from late 2026 to late 2027 (TRIS Rating, 2026, p. 2), but the MD&A lists Central GR9, which the team reads as The Grand Rama 9, for mid-2028, so the plan excludes it.

Chat & Shop (a LINE channel), Personal Shopper (a phone "Call & Shop" service) and in-store pick-up of online orders (a separate Drive Thru service in the 2021 release) were described in 2020 and 2021 as Central department-store services, not CPN services; their current status was not checked (Central Group, 2020, 2021). ### 4.4 Marketing Communications (Promotion)

This section serves M1, M2, M3, M4, M5 and F1. Incremental media weight goes to Gen Z, then tourists, while working adults and families (Primary) are reached through the flagship seasons and the opening campaigns. Marketing owns it, with Sustainability for M5 and the CRM team for The 1.

[Proposed] The core message is that CPN builds places where young people can do things that cannot be done online, such as playing sport, making things and meeting friends, and that it reports its environmental targets openly.

In October 2025 TikTok's advertising reach was 56.6 million adults, YouTube's 47.2 million people, and LINE had 56 million monthly users; these are not Gen Z-specific audiences, and reach is not unique users (DataReportal, 2025). CPN used KOLs and bloggers, over a hundred by its wording, for Pride For All 2026 (Central Pattana, 2026l). [Proposed] The team weights Gen Z media to TikTok, YouTube and LINE and ties every creator brief to a CENTRAL X registration so that attention becomes a visit.

By 17 October 2026 arrivals were 24.4 million, down 4.12%, with China at 4.14 million, Malaysia 3.10 million and India 1.84 million (Nation Thailand, 2026i); in January–August China was up 16.05%, Malaysia down 11.87%, India flat and South Korea down 24.78% (Nation Thailand, 2026g). TAT targets 850,000 Middle East arrivals and Riyadh Air began Riyadh–Bangkok flights in September (Nation Thailand, 2026h); the Middle East was reported down 24.9% earlier in the year (Nation Thailand, 2026e), although CPN's 2Q26 MD&A says May arrivals grew 4% as Middle East tourists returned, without giving a number (Central Pattana, 2026g, p. 1). India's reported ฿36,354 a trip, whose period the source does not state, is about 25% below overall revenue per arrival of about ฿48,700 for 1 January to 17 October 2026 (team calculation from Nation Thailand, 2026f, 2026i; periods differ, so indicative only), and the source's daily-spend and stay figures do not reconcile to it. M2 is therefore stage-gated: [Proposed] Gulf, India and Europe spending is released in tranches against the registrant data from Section 4.5.

[Proposed] Sustainability storytelling publishes three milestones, each tied to a CPN document and stated on its current basis: a carbon-intensity cut, free community space, and Net Zero by 2050 (Central Pattana, 2024b). The 2024 framework sets a 56% cut in Scope 1 and 2 carbon intensity by 2034 against 2019 and free community space of 7.0% of commercial space by 2030 (Central Pattana, 2024d). CPN's 2025 climate summary sets the base year at 2024 and a 2034 goal of a 50% cut in emissions intensity per unit area (Central Pattana, n.d.-g, pp. 1, 5). The same summary says the Board of Directors resolved to maintain a Scope 1 and 2 reduction target of 46.2%, and the passage reviewed gives no base year or target year for it (Central Pattana, n.d.-g, pp. 5–6), so three different targets appear in CPN's documents: 56% against 2019, 50% against 2024 and 46.2%. The free-space measure is now total leasable area times days for 43 malls, 2.4% in 2025 (Central Pattana, n.d.-b, pp. 3–4). The team quotes the 50% cut by 2034 against 2024, the target in CPN's latest climate summary, and does not quote the 56% (2019 base) or the 46.2% (no stated base year) to customers; it publishes only against the current definitions.

CPN's finance report gives 62.8 kgCO2e per square metre for 2025 against a path value of 77, before reallocating chilled-water energy to tenants (272,520 tCO2e over 4.34 million square metres; Central Pattana, n.d.-b, pp. 2, 5). The climate summary restates 2025 Scope 1 and 2 intensity to 42.8, and 2024 to 47.5, after that reallocation, which moves emissions out of Scope 2 (Central Pattana, n.d.-g, pp. 5, 7). The team will not present the gain as an operating achievement without naming the basis. CPN says its targets are aligned with the SBTi methodology but states no SBTi validation and says it awaits clarity on international real estate criteria, so the words science-based are avoided (Central Pattana, n.d.-g, pp. 5–6; Central Pattana Investor Relations, 2026, p. 72). The ฿8 billion bond supports clean-energy projects, so it is never described as funding the ฿110 billion programme (Central Group, 2026; Central Pattana, 2024b).

### 4.5 Marketing Research

This section serves M1 to M5 and F1. The first waves target Gen Z and tourists because their baselines are missing, while Primary-segment cohorts are read from The 1 extracts (S2). Research and insights own it, with the CRM team, the Data Protection Office and Sustainability.

In the FY2025 One Report (a team text search finds no mention of Gen Z), the 2Q26 MD&A and the releases reviewed, CPN publishes no Gen Z share of footfall, age split of The 1 members, definition of a visit, CENTRAL X user count or definition of Gen Z (Central Pattana, 2026a, 2026n; Central Pattana Investor Relations, 2026).

[Proposed] Age bands, defined once for the plan. The plan defines Gen Z and young adults as ages 18 to 29 (a team definition, not CPN's) and measures their visits in two ways. The intercept survey reaches only respondents aged 20 and over (the age of majority), whom the team treats as able to consent alone under the PDPA (younger respondents would need a guardian, s.20), so the plan uses it for ages 20 to 29. The 1 member data cover active members aged 18 to 29, so ages 18 and 19 are seen only there, and only as members (their visits as non-members are not measured). The only published comparator is DataReportal's 14.7% share of the population aged 13 to 24 (team sum of the 13–17 and 18–24 bands; DataReportal, 2025). That band is different from the plan band: it is wider at the young end, because it includes ages 13 to 17, whom the survey does not reach, and narrower at the old end, because it stops at 24 while the plan band runs to 29, so any comparison with it is indicative only. Table 5.2 row C1's 25% is a team assumption for the 18–29 band's share of visits. Research row S2 and Chapter 6 indicator K2 use the 18 to 29 band; research row S4 and indicator K1 use its 20 to 29 part, as a share of visitors aged 20 and over. Age bands above 29 are read beside both as a comparison.

[Proposed] The team builds the baseline between 1 January and 31 March 2027. The1 Central Limited, which its privacy policy names as the company collecting the data (The1 Central Limited, n.d.-a), supplies aggregated outputs by age band (active members, visits and spend per member, CENTRAL X scans), which the team compares with the 14.7% population share of ages 13–24 as an indicative comparator only, for the reasons above. Five pilot centres supply entrance-counter totals and CPN's definition of a visit. An intercept survey of 1,500 respondents aged 20 and over, 300 per centre, is weighted to the footfall profile; its margin of error is about ±2.5 points at a 50% estimate, and the smallest detectable change between waves is about 3.6 points at a 50% base share and about 3.1 points at a 25% base share, borrowed for illustration from the team's assumed 18 to 29 share of visits in Table 5.2 row C1 and not an estimate of K1 (team calculation, simple random sampling, so minimums). The policy names the Data Protection Office, Central Group, as its contact, and whether The1 Central Limited is the data controller is left to the Thai-law reader below.

[Proposed] Tourist research uses The 1 Tourist registrations as a self-selected panel, recording nationality at registration if it is not already recorded (Central Pattana, 2026k), aggregated spend by nationality from payment partners CPN names (WeChat Pay, Alipay and MasterCard; whether they can supply nationality-level spend is an assumption to test) (Central Pattana, 2025g), and exit surveys of 150 respondents per market group at six centres (±8 points per group, diagnostic only; team calculation), benchmarked against TAT figures (Nation Thailand, 2026f, 2026h).

[Proposed] The M5 tracker runs one wave a quarter with 400 respondents per target segment, asking an open unaided question on which developers come to mind for caring about the environment. A vendor specification uses 300 respondents as the minimum and calls quarterly waves the most common cadence (Standard Insights, 2026). At 400 respondents the margin is ±4.9 points at 50% and the smallest detectable wave-to-wave change is 6.9 points (team calculation), so M5 targets are set above that after wave 0.

[Proposed] The team read the Thai Royal Gazette text of the Personal Data Protection Act; no official English translation was found, so a Thai-law reader should confirm these points. Consent must be explicit and separate from other text (s.19), minors who cannot consent alone need a guardian (s.20), data collected from another source need notice within 30 days and consent (s.25), objections to direct marketing must be honoured (s.32), breaches are reported within 72 hours (s.37), and administrative fines reach ฿1 million, ฿3 million or ฿5 million by tier (Personal Data Protection Act, B.E. 2562, 2019). The PDPC fined a computer trading company ฿7 million in 2025 for weak security, a missing breach report and no data protection officer (Tilleke & Gibbins, 2025). CPN's CCTV notice lists security purposes, not people counting (Central Pattana, n.d.-a). The intercept survey reaches only respondents aged 20 and over, in every wave, whom the team treats as able to consent alone (the age of majority under the Civil and Commercial Code; s.20 asks for a guardian for minors).
