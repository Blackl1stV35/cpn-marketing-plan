## 5. Financials

This chapter tests whether the Chapter 4 plan can repay its cost and whether CPN can carry it. FY2027 is the plan year, FY2026E the base year (6M26 actual plus a 2H26E estimate) and FY2028E the carry-over. Every forecast is a team proposal that a Python script computes from Table 5.2 (the script is kept with the team's working files), not company guidance. CPN has reported only to 2Q26. Its calendar lists a 3Q26 earnings call on 16 November 2026, organised by SET (CPN's own call is not yet listed; Central Pattana Investor Relations, n.d.-a), but CPN has not announced the date of its 3Q26 MD&A filing (none was listed on 28 October 2026; Central Pattana Investor Relations, n.d.-b), so the model is rerun when the MD&A appears. In each of the last two quarters CPN filed its MD&A before its own earnings call: the 2Q26 MD&A on 10 August, 4 days before the call on 14 August, and the 1Q26 MD&A on 15 May, 3 days before the call on 18 May (Central Pattana, 2026g; Central Pattana Investor Relations, n.d.-a, n.d.-b). ### 5.1 Break-even Analysis

Required extra revenue equals incremental programme cost divided by the contribution-margin ratio, the share of each extra baht of revenue left after the costs that rise with it. The MD&A and One Report we read give no fixed and variable cost split, so the team brackets the ratio with three readings of its FY2025 figures (Table 5.2 row D6): 42.6% if every cost varies, 60.0% if only the cost of rental and services varies, and 86.3% if only utilities vary (Central Pattana, 2026p; Central Pattana Investor Relations, 2026). The base reading is cautious only if depreciation sits in the cost of rental and services, which the One Report does not state (team inference, not verified).

The incremental cost is not the ฿2,760 million ceiling. The team assumes the flagship windows cost what their latest disclosed editions cost: Great Chinese New Year 2026 (฿600 million, joint with Central Retail, split not stated), Summer Fest 2026 (฿600 million) and the 2025 Magical Stars season (over ฿800 million, reported as CPN's own spend; the article names Disney and TAT as collaborators) (Central Pattana, 2026s; Nation Thailand, 2025c; Thai Publica, 2026), and that The 1 BIZ stays at the ฿200 million a year of platform marketing investment in the 2024 release (funder not stated; the team assumes CPN funds an equal amount) (Central Pattana, 2024e). Those ฿2,200 million are assumed to sit in the FY2026E cost base, leaving ฿560 million net-new (Table 5.9). That is an upper bound on what is embedded, since CPN discloses no marketing expense; each ฿100 million not in the base lowers the FY2027E margin by about 0.14 points (team calculation). The ฿560 million splits into ฿240 million for the tactics that carry M1 to M4 uplift (lines 4, 6, 7 and 9 less the ฿100 million line 1 cut) and ฿320 million for opening launches, sustainability storytelling and research, which earn no revenue credit.

### 5.2 Sales Forecast

FY2026E is 6M26 actual plus a 2H26E estimate built from 2H25 (Table 5.1), with the ฿1,234 million one-off removed from other income. The base case grows R&S revenue 7.5% in 2H26E, the 2Q26 rate (Central Pattana, 2026g), so FY2026E R&S revenue grows 7.1%, inside CPN's high-single-digit guidance for rental and services (Central Pattana, 2026q, slide 15). This adopts the guidance rather than testing it: at the 6M26 rate (+6.7%) in 2H26E, FY2026E R&S growth would be +6.7%, under the 7.0% definition, and the base case needs at least +7.3%. Total revenue grows only 4.6% because the FY2025 base holds the one-off; against FY2025 core revenue of ฿51,775 million (Central Pattana, 2026p) it grows 7.1%.

The conservative case stresses growth, costs and delivery together: R&S grows 5.0% in 2H26E and 4.0% a year after, as the Bank of Thailand projects growth to slow from 2.4% in 2026 to 2.1% in 2027 (Bank of Thailand, 2026). The base case grows R&S organically 6.5% and 6.0%, below CPN's high-single-digit revenue target for 2026 to 2030 (Central Pattana, 2026q, slide 16). The optimistic case is defined as meeting the target, so its pass is by construction and it is not a forecast (Table 5.6).
