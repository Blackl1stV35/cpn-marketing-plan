Q1 Why back a plan that misses F1
and does not repay?
Spkr 6
A gated test. The no-plan path also misses F1 (6.0%
growth, 35.2% margin). Only ฿240m of tactics repay;
benefits after FY2028E are not valued.
Q2 Does the plan pay for itself?
Spkr 4
No. On assumed uplift, ฿560m a year is not repaid by
FY2028E (needs 67% pass-through). ฿240m of tactics
repay in 1.2 years; ฿320m earns none.
Q3 Isn't our 35.5% floor inflated by
one-offs? Is F1 missed on core?
Spkr 5
Yes: FY2025 held ฿2,119m of Rama 2
one-offs (3.2 points). On the K8 core test, base 33.9%
clears a 32.3% floor, but growth of 6.6% still misses
7.0%.
Q4 What evidence supports the
extra revenue?
Spkr 4
Not yet measured. Team assumptions (25% visit share
for ages 18–29, +10% frequency, +5% lift) carry 79%
of FY2028E uplift. The baseline and gates G2 to G4
test the frequency and lift; the 25% share is not
measured directly, as K1 is the 20–29 share of visitors
aged 20+.
Q5 How do you know ฿2,200m is
already in costs?
Spkr 1
We do not. CPN reports no separate marketing
expense, so ฿2,200m is our assumption. Each ฿100m
not in the base cuts FY2027E margin by 0.14 points.
Q6 How much of the money really
depends on results?
Spkr 5
45% of net-new (฿250m of ฿560m) waits for a results
test, at G2 or G3. G0 and G1 test readiness;
฿310m (55%) is due by 30 Jun.
Q7 What stops a loss-making
FY2028 repeat?
Spkr 5
G4 requires ฿400m uplift and pass-through above
29% (tactics break-even); a miss limits FY2028 to
tactics that passed. The full ฿560m needs ฿933m and
67%: reviewed, not required.
Sustainability, data and definitions
Q8 What is the ESG index called,
and is FTSE4Good confirmed?
Spkr 2
The Dow Jones Best-in-Class Indices (formerly DJSI):
World 8 years, Emerging Markets 12, per CPN,
9 Apr 2026. FTSE4Good: per CPN, unconfirmed.
Q9 Which emissions target do you
quote?
Spkr 6
One only: a 50% cut in emissions intensity by 2034
against a 2024 base, the target in CPN's latest (2025)
climate summary.
Q10 How many members does
The 1 have?
Spkr 2
Central Retail's loyalty ecosystem: 23 million members
in Thailand (press release, 14 Aug 2026). It does not
name the programme; we read it as The 1.
Q11 Where does the 1Q27 date for
The Central Phaholyothin come
from?
Spkr 3
CPN's 2Q26 MD&A (p. 7) lists "The Central" at 1Q27,
which we read as The Central Phaholyothin. Spend
waits for a CPN filing (D1); other dates on B2.
Q12 Which net margin is right:
35.5% or 33.1%?
Spkr 5
35.5% is our calculation (18,841 / 53,009). CPN
publishes 33.1% (IR page), about 35.6% (MD&A) and
36% (One Report). We never call 35.5% CPN's figure.
Q13 How is Gen Z defined, and
what consent age?
Spkr 4
Ages 18–29 (team definition). The survey reaches only
people aged 20+ (age of majority; younger people
would need a guardian on our reading of PDPA s.20),
so it measures 20–29; member data cover active
members 18–29. DataReportal's 13–24 is a different
band.
