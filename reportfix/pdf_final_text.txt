Type and shapes

en dash.

tiles 25; bullets 26 to 31; table text 19 to 30 on core slides (14 to 17 on reference pages) with a bold header row; card titles 28 to 38 bold; chart labels 19
to 22; sources and navigation 20. Smallest body text on core slides is 18.
Corner radii 14 to 20 px on cards, pills fully rounded; outlines 2 px (3 px on highlighted boxes).
Charts (S8, S9)
Build as native, editable charts. Value above each column; where a value would sit on a dashed reference line it goes inside the column top in white.
Reference lines are dashed, full plot width, with the value at the right end in the line colour (gold text uses gold-dark #855C00).
Category notes are pale-yellow chips under the category label. When an axis does not start at zero (S9) the note 'Axis starts at X%' sits under the axis at
the left. Series order and colours are listed in each slide's text block.
Labels used on slides
[Proposed] marks team proposals; 'team assumption', 'team calculation', 'team assessment' mark our own figures; 'per CPN' marks figures reported by
CPN. NEW means built for the final deck; RECAP means reworked from the 7 Sep deck.

depends on results?
