# Proposed change: the measurement band (29 Oct 2026)

Project: Marketing Business Economics (Kasetsart); marketing plan for Central Pattana (CPN); 10-minute final talk plus Q&A.
Decision already taken by the team lead: the PDPA consent age is 20 (Civil Code age of majority). The intercept survey therefore reaches only respondents aged 20 and over; younger respondents would need a guardian (PDPA s.20). The plan's segment "Gen Z and young adults" is defined as ages 18 to 29 (a team definition, not CPN's).

PROBLEM: the report said the survey indicator K1 is the "Gen Z and young-adult (18-29) share of footfall", but a survey of people aged 20+ cannot see ages 18 and 19, and it cannot see visitors under 20 at all.

DECISION (team lead: "proceed per your recommendation"): the survey-based indicator K1 becomes the 20-29 share; ages 18-29 are kept only for The 1 member data (K2). The planning segment (effort allocation, objective M1, model row C1 with its 25% visit-share assumption, slides S2 and S4) stays "ages 18-29". No number in the report or the model changes; only definitions and wording.

Terms: K1 = Chapter 6 indicator, survey share, baseline due 31 Mar 2027, target +4 points by the Q4 wave, smallest detectable change 3.6 points at a 50% base and 3.1 points at a 25% base (n = 1,500, simple random sampling, team calculation). K2 = The 1 member data, visits per active member aged 18-29, target +10% by Q4. Table 5.2 row C1 = model row for objective M1: 25% of visits (team assumption for the 18-29 band) x +10% frequency x 50% yield = 1.25% of organic revenue and sales.
