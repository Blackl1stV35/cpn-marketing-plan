[EDITED 195] [Proposed] Age bands, defined once for the plan. The plan measures Gen Z and young adults as ages 18 to 29 (a team definition, not CPN's); the surveys reach only respondents aged 20 and over, whom the team treats as able to consent alone under the PDPA, and younger respondents would need a guardian (s.20). The only published comparator is DataReportal's 14.7% share of the population aged 13 to 24 (team sum of the 13–17 and 18–24 bands; DataReportal, 2025). That band is different from the plan band: it is wider at the young end, because it includes ages 13 to 17, whom the survey does not reach, and narrower at the old end, because it stops at 24 while the plan band runs to 29, so any comparison with it is indicative only. Table 5.2 row C1's 25% is a team assumption for the 18–29 band's share of visits. S2, S4 and Chapter 6 indicators K1 and K2 use this band, and age bands above 29 are read beside it as a comparison.
