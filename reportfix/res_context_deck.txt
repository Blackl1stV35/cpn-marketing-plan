We propose releasing money in five gates from 15 December 2026. Net-new spend is capped at 560 million baht. A failed gate
holds or cuts the next release. Gates zero and one test readiness: set-up and baseline. From gate two, we test results. Gate zero
releases the first 155 million baht for Gen Z, tourist and CENTRAL X programmes. Gates one and two release the second half; gate
three, the year-end season. Gate four, on 31 January 2028, decides the 2028 repeat. A proposed Plan Office under CPN's chief
marketing officer owns the dashboard and the gates. Our sixth speaker covers the risks.

We answer the 13 likely questions plainly, with a suggested speaker for each: the
base case misses F1 and the extra revenue is our assumption.
Sources: Central Pattana, 2026a, 2026g (p. 7), 2026p, 2026t, n.d.-g (pp. 1, 5); Central Pattana Investor Relations, n.d.-c, 2026; Central Retail Corporation, 2026;

Plan and numbers
Q1 Why back a plan that misses F1 and
does not repay?
Spkr 6
A gated test. The no-plan path also misses F1 (6.0%
growth, 35.2% margin). Only ฿240m of tactics repay;
benefits after FY2028E are not valued.
Q2 Does the plan pay for itself?
Spkr 4
No. On assumed uplift, ฿560m a year is not repaid by
FY2028E (needs 67% pass-through). ฿240m of tactics repay
in 1.2 years; ฿320m earns none.
Q3 Isn't our 35.5% floor inflated by
one-offs? Is F1 missed on core?
Spkr 5
Yes: FY2025 held ฿2,119m of Rama 2 one-offs (3.2 points).
On the K8 core test, base 33.9% clears a 32.3% floor, but
growth of 6.6% still misses 7.0%.
Q4 What evidence supports the extra
revenue?
Spkr 4
Not yet measured. Team assumptions (25% visit share for
ages 18–29, +10% frequency, +5% lift) carry 79% of
FY2028E uplift. Gates G2 and G3 test the lift and the
active-member frequency (at least +5% each) and G4 the
uplift they produce; the 25% share is not measured directly,
as K1 is the 20–29 share of visitors aged 20+.
Q5 How do you know ฿2,200m is
already in costs?
Spkr 1
We do not. CPN reports no separate marketing expense, so
฿2,200m is our assumption. Each ฿100m not in the base
cuts FY2027E margin by 0.14 points.
Q6 How much of the money really
depends on results?
Spkr 5
45% of net-new (฿250m of ฿560m) waits for a results test,
at G2 or G3. G0 and G1 test readiness; ฿310m (55%) is due
by 30 Jun.
Q7 What stops a loss-making FY2028
repeat?
Spkr 5
Gate G4, but only in part. It requires the tactics break-even
(฿400m uplift, pass-through above 29%). The full ฿560m
needs ฿933m uplift and 67% pass-through; G4 reports that
test but does not require it, so a pass can release a repeat
the base case does not repay (FY2028E uplift ฿696m). A
miss limits FY2028 to the tactics that passed.
Sustainability, data and definitions
Q8 What is the ESG index called, and is
FTSE4Good confirmed?
Spkr 2
The Dow Jones Best-in-Class Indices (formerly DJSI):
World 8 years, Emerging Markets 12, per CPN, 9 Apr 2026.
FTSE4Good: per CPN, unconfirmed.
Q9 Which emissions target do you
quote?
Spkr 6
One only: a 50% cut in emissions intensity by 2034 against
a 2024 base, the target in CPN's latest (2025) climate
summary.
Q10 How many members does The 1
have?
Spkr 2
Central Retail's loyalty ecosystem: 23 million members in
Thailand (press release, 14 Aug 2026). It does not name the
programme; we read it as The 1.
Q11 Where does the 1Q27 date for The
Central Phaholyothin come from?
Spkr 3
CPN's 2Q26 MD&A (p. 7) lists "The Central" at 1Q27, which
we read as The Central Phaholyothin. Spend waits for a
CPN filing (D1); other dates on B2.
Q12 Which net margin is right: 35.5% or
33.1%?
Spkr 5
35.5% is our calculation (18,841 / 53,009). CPN publishes
33.1% (IR page), about 35.6% (MD&A) and
36% (One Report). We never call 35.5% CPN's figure.
Q13 How is Gen Z defined, and what
consent age?
Spkr 4
Ages 18–29 (team definition). The survey reaches only
people aged 20+ (age of majority; younger people would
need a guardian on our reading of PDPA s.20), so it
measures 20–29; member data cover active members
18–29 (18–19 provisional). DataReportal's 13–24 is a
different band.
